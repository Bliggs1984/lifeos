Define your first real learning track

Payoff: tomorrow morning the card waiting for you is about something you actually want to learn, built from a source your assistant fetched and checked, pointed at a thing you will be able to hold up at the end. This is the moment the template becomes yours.

01What a good track looks like

A track is not a subject. It is a route to an artifact: something that exists afterwards and that you could show someone.

Weak trackStrong trackWhy
"Learn Python""A script that renames my photo library by date" (5 sessions)You can run it. You know when you are done.
"Get better at cooking""Five weeknight dinners I can make without a recipe" (5 sessions)Countable, tastable, done.
"Understand AI""A one-page point of view I could defend in a meeting" (4 sessions)A document exists. Opinions are checkable.
why artifactsThe dots under the card only mean something if they count toward a thing. "3 of 5 sessions in Python" is a number. "3 of 5 toward a working script" is a promise. The second one gets you out of bed.

02Write the mission

with your assistant, 10 min
Open learning/MISSION.md. It has a template. Answer the one hard question: what changes in your life or work when you have this? If the honest answer is "I would just like to know it", push once more. There is usually a concrete outcome underneath.

Then agree one track: name, artifact, number of sessions (four to six is right). Add it to tracks in data/learning.json. Your assistant knows the schema.

03Constraints that make cards land

Every card the curation builds obeys these, and you should hold it to them:

ConstraintWhy
Twenty to thirty minutesLonger than that and the card gets swapped, not done.
Payoff in the first lineIf the reason to do it is at the bottom, nobody reaches the bottom.
One tangible win per cardSomething exists or works at the end. Reading alone does not count.
A primary source, fetched and verifiedThe assistant reads the actual page and links it. Never recalled from memory.
A quiz or a checkA feedback loop, however small, is what makes it stick.

04Deal the first hand

Run the curate ritual (/curate in Claude Code, or "run the weekly curation from CLAUDE.md" elsewhere). It will propose five to seven cards plus two wildcards. Strike what you do not want. It builds lessons for the rest and puts them in the queue.

When it is done, press Done on this card, write your takeaway, and retire the Getting started track by setting its status to "done" in learning.json. Tomorrow's card is yours.

05Check it stuck
